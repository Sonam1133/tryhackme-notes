## Room 1: Junior Security Analyst Intro

**Path:** SOC Level 1 > Blue Team Introduction

**What I did:**
Worked through a simulated day as a junior SOC analyst. I reviewed
security alerts, investigated traffic from a malicious IP address,
escalated the incident to the right team, and blocked the IP with a
firewall rule.

**Key takeaways:**
- **Triage first:** Not every alert is an attack. Check the evidence
  before acting.
- **Escalate correctly:** Analysts hand incidents to the right team
  rather than handling everything alone.
- **Contain the threat:** Blocking a malicious IP at the firewall stops
  further traffic from it.
- **Verify the fix:** After blocking, confirm the rule is active and the
  malicious traffic has stopped. Don't assume it worked.
- **Document everything:** Clear incident notes let the next analyst
  understand what happened and why.

**Skills practiced:** alert triage, incident escalation, firewall
blocking, incident documentation
